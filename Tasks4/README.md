# Financial Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive **Financial Performance Dashboard** designed to analyze key business metrics such as Sales, Profit, Cost, Quantity, and Margin.
The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze overall financial performance
* Identify top-performing products
* Understand segment-wise sales distribution
* Evaluate country-level performance
* Study sales trends over time
* Measure the impact of discount strategies

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI (Dashboard & Data Visualization)
* Dataset: Financial Data

---

## Dashboard Highlights

### KPI Cards

Provide a high-level snapshot of overall business performance, including Total Sales, Profit, Cost, Quantity, and Margin.

### Sales and Profit by Product

Compares revenue and profitability across products to identify high-performing and underperforming items.

### Quantity and Profit by Product

Displays the relationship between sales volume and profit contribution.

### Sales by Segment

Shows how different customer segments contribute to total sales.

### Units Sold by Country

Highlights geographic performance based on sales volume.

### Sales Trend Over Time

Analyzes monthly performance patterns and seasonality.

### Discount Band Analysis

Evaluates the impact of discount levels on Sales and Profit.

---

## Key Insights

* Sales performance varies significantly across products and segments
* Certain products drive higher profit margins despite lower sales volume
* Discount strategies influence both revenue and profitability
* Seasonal trends are visible in monthly sales patterns
* Country-level analysis reveals regional strengths and opportunities

---

## Outcome

The dashboard provides clear visibility into financial performance and supports better strategic planning.
